Oakdale, TN Weather Forecast

Tonight: Clear and cold with a low near 43 degrees. Winds light and variable out of the northwest at 3 to 6 miles per hour. Chance of rain 10 percent.

Sunday: Sunny skies with a high near 68 degrees. A few afternoon clouds will build over the plateau, but no rain is expected during the day. Chance of rain 10 percent.

Sunday Night: Partly cloudy early, then clearing after midnight. Low around 46 degrees with light winds. Chance of rain 20 percent toward daybreak Monday.

Thursday: Sunny and mild. High near 66. Low near 44. Chance of rain 0 percent.

Friday: Increasing clouds late. High near 67. A chance of showers Friday night. Chance of rain 40 percent.
